## Scope

Severity 2:

1. **The character card can't be folded again on a phone.** Once opened under 900 px, it stays open. Its head shows only while folded (map 13's amendment), so the open card has nothing to fold it with. The open card gets a fold control, a small "Fold" button at its top that's at least 44 px tall. Focus goes back to the card's head when it folds.
2. **A list opens with no rows after a reload at 375 px** until something is typed in its filter.
   - The likely cause: `WindowedRows` measures its viewport while the list is still hidden or has zero height, and never measures again.
   - Measure on open, or with a `ResizeObserver` on the viewport, so the first paint has rows.
   - Test a list opened straight after load.
3. **The trail opens the background before the deity,** though the deity sits above it on the card.
   - The trail follows the card's order, top to bottom: race, deity, background, alignment.
   - Check that auto-advance and "next step" agree after each pick.

Severity 1:

4. **The Undo line and the chips word nested picks differently.** Both use `nestedLabel`, so a nested pick reads the same in every place.
5. **The level editor's title and subheading don't match.** Make them name the same thing: the stop's level and the class or archetype held, as the stop reads, for example "Two-Handed Fighter 1".
6. **Archetypes are scattered through the first class list.** Each class comes with its archetypes right under it ("Two-Handed Fighter · Fighter"), and the classes keep their order. A filter keeps a class's archetypes together under it, and still matches either name.

## Outcome

Planner UI, 2026-11-23, on `ticket/trail-qa-1122`. Each finding has a test that failed before its fix, and each was checked in the browser at 375 and 1280 px.

1. **The card folds again on a phone.**
   - The open card carries a "Fold" button at its top, 44 px tall, right-aligned and marked `aria-expanded="true"`.
   - Folding hands focus back to the card's head.
   - At 900 px and wider the card isn't foldable, so it has no button.
2. **An empty list after a reload.** I couldn't make it happen with the tab in front: every list filled after a reload at 375 and at 1280.
   - The code had one way to an empty window, and it matches "until something is typed": a list whose measured scroll runs past its fewer rows. `windowOf` put the start at the item count, and typing fixed it because typing resets `scrollTop`.
   - `windowOf` now clamps its top to the rows it has, and `WindowedRows` measures again as soon as its rows change.
   - QA pass 4's `windowOf` test had asserted the empty window, `{ start: 1, end: 1 }` for one row scrolled past its end. It now expects the row.
3. **Deity before background.** The trail follows the card: the deity, then the background. Auto-advance and "next step" agree, since both go through `nextStep`.
   - Alignment still comes before the deity, because a deity's options depend on it (QA pass 5).
   - The tests that walked background then deity now walk deity then background.
4. **The nested-pick wording.** The Undo line uses `nestedLabel` of `shownName`s, as the chips do.
   - Repro: Jack's level 7 Bloodline Feat, which the list qualifies "(Fey Bloodline)". It read "Picked Bloodline Feat Selection (Fey Bloodline) — Dodge"; it now reads "Picked Bloodline Feat (Dodge)", like its chip.
   - This is one string in `PickStep`'s `takeNested`, which ticket 13 owns, so expect a small conflict there.
5. **The level editor.** Its title is "Level N · <stop>" and its subheading is "A class or archetype in place of <stop>". Both use `levelStopName`, which the stop's own button uses too.
6. **Archetypes under their class.**
   - Class rows carry a `group`. `groupedFirst` keeps each class's place and puts its archetypes right under it, and `rankedRows` ranks a group by its best match while keeping its members together.
   - "two-handed fighter" + Enter still takes the archetype.
