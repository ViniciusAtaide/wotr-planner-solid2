# Synergy link kinds in v1

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 03
Parent: [map](../map.md)

## Question

Which kinds of Synergy link does v1 derive, and in what order? Candidates:
- (i) grants or meets something another pick requires
- (ii) same weapon category or group
- (iii) same spell school or spell type
- (iv) same ability score feeding attack rolls, save DCs or uses per day
- (v) same condition or effect type
- (vi) a bonus type already in the Build, so the two won't stack: a warning, not a link

Proposed: all six, with (v) last if its reader is costly. The component research decides what each one costs.

## Answer

User, 2026-11-19: the cheap kinds only, with the expensive ones removed. Priced in [component-vocabulary.md](../research/component-vocabulary.md).

- **v1 link kinds:**
  - prerequisite chain (this enables / this needs), from the grant index;
  - ability score;
  - weapon category or group, including the weapon picked for Weapon Focus-style feats;
  - spell school, including Spell Focus-style picks;
  - energy type;
  - a short hand-picked descriptor list (fear, mind-affecting and a few more).
- **Not links:** a shared bonus type is a "won't stack" warning instead.
- **Removed:** critical hits, flanking and other conditions. They aren't links in v1, not even through hand-written summary tags. A hand-written summary may still describe them in words.
