# The walk (W)

W is the single judge of legality (guide §4). `walk` makes one pass over a Build, level-up by level-up, each in the game's band order (`BANDS`, the game's LevelUpActionPriority), with Mythic ranks at their Rank levels (ADR 0003). It records every fact the character holds with its source and place, every slot it opens with its pick, and every choice that fails. P (`engine/prerequisites`) says what each prerequisite leaf asks and G (`engine/grants`) says who grants what; the walk answers whether the character has it and applies the grants. Its tests run on the committed data (ticket 03; guide §5 step 6).

Every rule here ports one decompiled C# method. `cites.ts` holds the citations, keyed by the identifier that ports each rule; paths there are relative to `decompiled/Assembly-CSharp/Kingmaker/` at WrathDecompiled 6ce4a6b, and most level-up actions live in `UnitLogic/Class/LevelUp/Actions/`. A game rule's name in this file is its C# class and method (`SelectClass.Check`); the prose is ours.

## Public entry

- **The walk** (`walk.ts`): `walk(data, build, G?)` returns a `Walk`: its `gains`, `failures`, opened `slots`, `gearDependent` choices, path-switch `dropped` facts, each level's `attributePoints` and `skillPoints`, the finished `unit`, `maxCharacterLevel` (20, or 40 once a fact sets `LegendaryHero`: `UnitProgressionData.MaxCharacterLevel`, the same cap that refuses a level or rank above it), and `ranksEnded`: true once a taken rank ends the ascensions (Legend), the same flag that refuses any rank after it (`map31.legendEndsAscensions`). `pauseAt(data, build, slot, G?)` returns a `PausedWalk` (build ticket 04). `pauseAtClass(data, build, place, G?, rankLevel?)` returns a `PausedClassChoice`, the walk paused at a level's or rank's class choice, whose `judge(cls)` gives every refusal the walk would record for that class there, and whose `judgeArchetype(cls, archetype)` gives the archetype's refusal as `addArchetypes` would make it with the class newly taken. `addArchetypes` and the pause share `archetypeRefusal` (belongs to the class, at its first level, fits beside the class's other archetypes, prerequisites). A new level or rank pauses where the walk would reach it. The checks live in one method, `classRefusals`, which the walk's own `selectClass` records, so the pause and the walk can't disagree. They cover the class kind, the Legend choice class, a path switch off rank 8, `MeetsPrerequisites`, the level cap, ranks ended and Devil's origin. Also `BANDS` and `Band`, `bandOf`, the result types `Gain`, `OpenedSlot`, `GearDependent`, `Dropped`, and `appliedGrantTypes` and `appliedFieldGrants` for the coverage check.
- **The character** (`unit.ts`): `Unit`, `Fact`, `Source`, `ClassData`, `ProgressionData`, the parameter helpers `paramKey`, `paramBlueprint`, `paramValue`, `isParamType` and `ParamType`, `ATTRIBUTES`, `SKILL_ATTRIBUTE`, `isAttribute`, `isSkill`, `stackingOf` and `applyProgression`.
- **Failures** (`failure.ts`, re-exported by `walk.ts`): `Rule`, `Value`, `Unmet`, `FailedRule`, `Failure`.
- **Stats** (`stats.ts`): `POINT_BUY`, `attributeBonus`, `pointBuyCost`, `skillPointsAvailable`, `applyStats`, `StatsHost`.
- **Spellbooks** (`spellbooks.ts`): `spellbookTypes`, `SpellbookData`, `demand`, `classSpellbook`, `applySpellbook`, `maxSpellLevel`, `hasCasterType`, `casterTypeSpellLevel`, `classSpellLevel`.
- **Weapons** (`weapons.ts`): `WEAPON_CATEGORIES`, `hasSubCategory`.
- **Areas and coverage** (`areas.ts`, `coverage.ts`): `walkAreas` and the type lists `selectionTypes`, `skillTypes`, `proficiencyTypes`, `abilityScoreTypes`, `aspectTypes`; `grantsAppliedElsewhere`, `readerProblems`, `grantCoverageProblems`.
- **Gear** (`gear.ts`): `PLACE_ITEM_TYPES`, the item types each Gear place takes, which the planner's Gear panel offers.
- `cites`, the citation registry.

Not exported from `index.ts` but imported directly by other modules and tests: `check.ts` (the prerequisite judge), `gear.ts`, `progression.ts`, `daily-choices.ts` and `test-data.ts`.

## Rules

### The walk (`walk.ts`)

- `BANDS`: the game's level-up bands, in the order a level-up applies its choices.
- `bandOf`: a pick's band from its slot's selection's group (`SelectFeature.CalculatePriority`): deities, heritages, backgrounds, Mythic Feat and Mythic Ability, the "path" groups (domains, bloodlines, schools, bonds, curses, spirits, mysteries, blessings and the like) and the spellbook groups each have their band; everything else is Features.
- `Gain`: a fact gained, or raised a rank, at a place, with the rank reached; what the gold builds compare against.
- `OpenedSlot`: a slot the walk opened (`LevelUpState.AddSelection`) with what fills it. `level` is the granting progression's level (`FeatureSelectionState.Level`); `parent` is the slot it is nested in.
- `GearDependent`: a choice allowed only because the Build's Gear counts (ADR 0002's opt-in): a pick, a class level or an archetype whose prerequisites fail without the worn Gear's Enhancement bonuses, or that rests on such a pick. `subject` is the picked feature, the class or the archetype. Its `items` are the worn ones whose bonus it needs: each raises a score a failing stat prerequisite reads, or is an item of the gear-dependent pick it rests on.
- `Dropped`: a fact a path switch took away (map ticket 31). It counted from `gainedAt` until the switch at `place`.
- `Walk.attributePoints`: the ability increases each character level offers, in level order: 1 at every 4th character level, else 0 (`SelectClass.Apply`), read as the level reaches its AddAttribute band. Mythic ranks add no entry.
- `Walk.skillPoints`: the skill points each character level offers, in level order (`ApplySkillPoints.Apply`'s `TotalSkillPoints`, the budget the game's level-up screen shows): `skillPointsAvailable` read as the level reaches its Skills band, before its own ranks are spent. It counts points left unspent at earlier levels, and is never below 1. Mythic ranks offer none and add no entry.
- `Walk.unit`: the character after the whole Build, who holds what and from where (G's Build-aware half).
- `SlotState`: a slot during its level-up (`FeatureSelectionState`). `LevelState`: one level-up's counters and open slots (`LevelUpState`): whether it is the first character level, the next class level and the attribute points.
- `Owner`: what applies facts and takes them away when it goes (`Walker.applied`): a held fact, a Gear place, or a toggle.
- The path switches' classes and what map ticket 31 has Devil drop are named constants: `DEVIL_MYTHIC_CLASS`, `LEGEND_CLASS`, `AEON_MYTHIC_CLASS`, `AZATA_MYTHIC_CLASS`. `SWITCH_TO` holds the switch classes beside Gold Dragon and Swarm That Walks (which the game marks `m_IsHigherMythic`). `DEVIL_DROPS` follows the Rust planner's devil.ron `dropped_features` (azata/dragon-companion, aeon/enforcing-gaze-extra-gaze) and adds the gaze feature itself: `DRAGON_COMPANION`, `AEON_GAZE_FEATURE`, `AEON_GAZE_FEATURE_EXTRA_GAZE`. `DEVIL_DROPPED_SELECTIONS` follows devil.ron `dropped_selections` (aeon/enforcing-gaze, -2 and -3): `AEON_GAZE_FIRST_SELECTION`, `AEON_GAZE_SECOND_SELECTION`, `AEON_GAZE_THIRD_SELECTION`.
- `ON_ACTIVATE`: the grant components the walk runs when their fact is gained (`activate`). `CONDITIONAL`: those it re-judges while their condition can change (`refresh`). `WORN_GRANTS`: the equipment grants it holds while their item is worn (`wear`).
- `appliedGrantTypes`: the grant component types the walk applies. Every type G reads (`grantTypes`) is one of these or is listed, with the module that applies it, in `coverage.ts`'s `grantsAppliedElsewhere`.
- `appliedFieldGrants`: the grants a blueprint's own fields make (G's `fieldGrantKinds`) that the walk applies; every such kind is one of these or is listed in `coverage.ts`. By kind: `root` is applied by `run` (the feats progression; the classes, Mythic classes and races on offer are the Build's own choices); `classProgression` and `classArchetype` by `selectClass` and `addArchetypes` (a class's progression, and the archetypes it can be taken with); `levelEntry`, `archetypeAdd` and `archetypeRemove` are merged by `progression.ts`'s `levelEntries` (ProgressionData.CalculateLevelEntries); `selectionOption` by `canSelect` (a selection's options); `raceFeature` by `selectRace`; `itemEnchantment` and `shieldPart` by `wear`, through the data's `enchantmentsOf` (a worn item's enchantments, and a shield's armour half and bash weapon, as `BlueprintItemShield.CollectEnchantments` collects them); `ActivatableBuff` by `keepAspectsOn` (the buff an aspect kept on applies, map ticket 32).
- `conditionalGrants`: a blueprint's conditional grants, each with its component index, read once per blueprint. `unapplied` is the switches' default: the walk lists a grant type it has no case for.
- `walk`: walks a Build through the game's level-up rules; `G` defaults to a new index over `data`.
- `pathSwitchClasses(data)`: the classes a path switch applies: every class the game marks `m_IsHigherMythic` (Gold Dragon, Swarm That Walks), plus `SWITCH_TO` (Devil, Legend). `engine/pickers` adds them to the Mythic classes on offer.
- `PausedWalk` and `pauseAt` (build ticket 04): the walk paused at one slot, run to the end of the slot's band at its place, with the band's other picks applied as the walk applies them and only the slot's own pick withdrawn; later bands don't count. Judging only reads the paused walk, so every candidate is judged on the same character. `slot` is the slot, open and empty; `band` the band its pick is judged in (`SelectFeature.CalculatePriority`); `unit` the character at the pause; `judge` is `SelectFeature.Check` for an option (undefined when the game allows it); `gearItems` gives, under the Gear opt-in, the worn items an allowed option needs (ADR 0002), undefined when it needs none. `pauseAt` returns undefined when the slot never opens; `Paused` is thrown to stop the walk at the pause point, and `Walker.pause` is the slot to pause at.

`Walker`, the walk's state:

- `gainFacts`: the fact each gain gained or raised, so a switch takes away that very fact.
- `applied`: what each owner applied, to take away when it goes, by key: a held fact's grant components (each component's Data `AppliedFact` or `AppliedFacts`, keyed by the component's index, or by G's kind for the grants applied through G); the item worn in a Gear place (its AddUnitFeatureEquipment and AddUnitFactEquipment facts, under `equipment`); and an aspect kept on, by its toggle (its buff, under `ActivatableBuff`).
- `gearDependentFacts`: the facts of the gear-dependent picks, each with the items it needs (ADR 0002). `dropped`: what a path switch took away (map ticket 31). `legendTaken`: Legend ends the ascensions. `aspectsOn`: each aspect kept on now, by its toggle, with the buff the toggle applies (map ticket 32); `applied` holds its fact. `levelState` is the level-up under way, which the `level` getter reads; `picksAt` holds the Build's picks by place key, in Build order. `refusals`: each pick's last refusal, which `refusePending` records for a pick the level-up never applied.
- `run`: every character first holds the root's feats progression (the game's UnitProgressionData constructor). Ranks must come in rank order, each at or after the one before (ADR 0003, rule `adr0003.rankOrder`); each rank is walked after its Rank level, and ranks placed after the Build's last level come after it, in rank order. A rank whose Rank level is above the character-level cap at the moment it is walked (`maxCharacterLevel`: 20, or 40 once LegendaryHero is held) is refused with `UnitProgressionData.MaxCharacterLevel` and still walked, so the rest of the Build is judged (`ascend`; ticket 05d). The Legend rank itself is walked before LegendaryHero is held, so it too must sit at level 20 or below. Then the Gear never worn is refused, and each aspect the Build keeps on that the character never holds (`map32.notHeld`).

Facts:

- `gain`: `FeatureCollection.AddFact`, then the new fact's grant components (`EntityFact.Activate`). With `rankFrom`, the fact then takes that feature's rank when it is held (`ContextActionAddFeature.RunAction` with `m_SetRankFrom`, through `Feature.SetRank`). When a Replace buff's held fact went (`Unit.attach`), what it gave goes with it, and the grant that gave the old one no longer holds it.
- `recordGain`: records that a fact was gained or raised a rank here (`gains`) and which fact it was (`gainFacts`).
- `record`: records that an owner (by its component's index or G's kind, or its key) applied a fact, to take it away when the owner goes. A component that ran but applied nothing is recorded too, so a conditional one doesn't run again. `appliedOf`: what an owner's component applied, if it has run.
- `lose`: `EntityFactsManager.Remove` of one fact (`Unit.remove`). When the fact leaves, each of its grant components' OnDeactivate takes away what it applied: AddFacts (its Clear), AddFeatureIfHasFact, AddFeatureOnClassLevel, AddFeatureParametrized, AddUnitFeatureEquipment and AddUnitFactEquipment (their `AppliedFact`), AddFeatureOnAlignment, AddFeatureOnSkill, AddParametrizedFeatures and FactSinglify (their `AppliedFacts`), BuffExtraEffects and the Gear unlocks (their buff or fact), and ActivatableAbility's buff. AddFeatureOnApply, KensaiChosenWeapon, AddFactContextActions and ActivateTrigger have no such method, so what they gave stays. A rank dropped leaves the fact and its grants (`Feature.RemoveRank` reapplies them). Returns every fact that left, this one first.
- `loseApplied`: takes away everything an owner applied, as it goes; returns every fact that left. `descendants`: a fact while held, then what it applied that is still held, and so on down, depth first, each once. `loseBlueprint`: `lose` for the first held fact of a blueprint (`UnitHelper.RemoveFact` by blueprint).
- `activate`: the grant components a fact runs when it is gained (each component's OnActivate); the conditional ones are judged in `refresh`. AddFacts gains each listed fact into its `AppliedFacts` (`AddFacts.UpdateFacts`; no written blueprint sets HasDifficultyRequirements). AddFeatureOnApply gains its feature once, for good. RemoveFeatureOnApply removes its feature by blueprint (`UnitHelper.RemoveFact`). FactSinglify removes every copy of the old facts and keeps one copy of each new one, into `AppliedFacts`. KensaiChosenWeapon gains the focus feat at the fact's own weapon. AddFactContextActions runs its Activated actions. ActivateTrigger runs its Actions when its Conditions hold (`ActivateTrigger.Trigger`). AddParametrizedFeatures (into `AppliedFeatures`) and AddFeatureParametrized (into `AppliedFact`) run through G, which reads each grant's parameter. AddSpellbookLevel adds one base level to its spellbook, once.
- `runActions`: an action list's grant actions (map ticket 23), through its Conditional trees (`Conditional.RunAction`). Which actions grant, and what, is G's one answer (`actionGrant`): a ContextActionApplyBuff grants only when Permanent (`ContextActionApplyBuff.RunAction` gives any other a duration), and a ContextActionAddFeature's feature takes its `m_SetRankFrom` feature's rank (`gain`).
- `refresh`: the grants that hold while a condition does, re-judged until nothing changes: AddFeatureOnClassLevel (from a class level, or below it), AddFeatureIfHasFact, AddFeatureOnAlignment, AddFeatureOnSkill, BuffExtraEffects and the Gear unlocks. The game re-runs each on the event its condition reads (a level gained, a fact added, an item equipped); the walk runs them all after every change that can move one. Each keeps what it applied, as its component's Data does, and applies again only once that is cleared. It reads `Unit.watchedFacts`, a snapshot, since gaining facts adds to the map and losing one may take others.
- `conditional`: one conditional grant, re-judged: applies what it should hold and hasn't applied, and takes away what it applied once it should no longer hold, where its component does; says whether anything changed. Its `single` helper serves the one-fact components: whether it should hold, the fact, and whether it is taken away once it shouldn't. By component:
  - AddFeatureOnClassLevel (`Apply`, `IsFeatureShouldBeApplied`), with the class level `classLevelFor` reads (`ReplaceCasterLevelOfAbility.CalculateClassLevel`, `CheckArchetype`).
  - AddFeatureIfHasFact (`Apply`): adds once the condition holds; only its OnDeactivate takes it away.
  - AddFeatureOnAlignment (`OnActivate`): every listed fact, into `AppliedFacts`, while the alignment has the component. A Build's alignment never changes, so `HandleAlignmentChange` never runs.
  - AddFeatureOnSkill (`OnActivate`, `HandleUnitGainLevel`): once the stat's permanent value reaches the minimum, each listed fact not held, into `AppliedFacts`.
  - BuffExtraEffects (`CheckAndReapply`, whose list no kept component sets; `ExceptionFact`; `HandleBuffDidRemoved`).
  - MonkNoArmorFeatureUnlock (`CheckEligibility`): no shield and no body armour.
  - MonkNoArmorAndMonkWeaponFeatureUnlock (`CheckEligibility`): for a Zen Archer, the main hand holds one of the bow types (RapidShotBuff, the in-play exception, is never on in a Build); for a Sohei, light or no armour, no shield and a monk weapon (`UnitPartWeaponTraining.IsSuitableWeapon`'s other weapons aren't read, so this is stricter than the game); otherwise no armour, no shield and a monk weapon, where an empty main hand is the unarmed strike, a monk weapon (`UnitBody.EmptyHandWeapon`).
  - HasArmorFeatureUnlock (`CheckEligibility`), with `RestrictionsHelper.CheckArmor`: the body armour's type, or its group as `ItemEntityArmor.ArmorType` reads it, mithral included.

Gear:

- `wornArmor`: body armour worn, whose type is armour (`RestrictionsHelper.CheckHasArmor`). `wornShield`: a shield in the off hand (`HandSlot.HasShield`).
- `wearGear`: the Build's Gear at this level-up: an item goes on once the character can wear it (`ItemEntity.CanBeEquippedBy`) and comes off, refused, if it no longer can. A worn item's enchantments hold their AddUnitFeatureEquipment and AddUnitFactEquipment facts (G's `itemEnchantment` and `shieldPart` grants). `misfit` refuses an item the data doesn't hold, so such an item is skipped here and refused in `refuseUnworn`.
- `wear`: `ItemEntity.OnDidEquipped`: the item's enchantments activate, and each AddUnitFeatureEquipment or AddUnitFactEquipment gives the wielder its fact (its OnActivate, into `AppliedFact`).
- `takeOff`: `ItemEntity.OnWillUnequip`: each equipment grant's OnDeactivate removes the fact it gave, and what that fact gave.
- `refuseUnworn`: the Gear the Build never got to wear, with why, judged on the finished character.
- `classLevelFor`: `ReplaceCasterLevelOfAbility.CalculateClassLevel`: the character level without a class, else the listed classes' levels; a class not taken adds nothing, whether it counts or not.

Level-ups:

- `levelUp`: one level-up, band by band, refreshing the conditional grants and applying the band's picks after each, then refusing the picks it never applied, keeping aspects on and recording the slots it opened. It throws `Paused` at the pause slot's band.
- `selectRace`: `SelectRace.Check` and `Apply`: the race, its fact, and its features at level 0. It refuses a race some chosen archetype rules out (`BlueprintRace.GetRestrictedByArchetype`).
- `raceRefusal(data, build, race)`: the race choice's refusal, which `selectRace` records and `engine/pickers`' `racePicker` shows per row: not a race (`SelectRace.Check`), or ruled out by an archetype the Build takes (`restrictsRace`, `BlueprintRace.GetRestrictedByArchetype`: the archetype requires, by a PrerequisiteFeature, a race other than this one).
- `selectClass`: `SelectClass.Check` and `Apply`, with `UnitProgressionData.AddClassLevel`. A Mythic rank's class must be Mythic and a character level's must not (`SelectClass.Check:IsMythic`). A character level past `maxCharacterLevel` is refused (`UnitProgressionData.MaxCharacterLevel`, via `CanLevelUpNormal`). A refused class still takes its level, so the rest of the Build is judged. A class taken for the first time gets its own spellbook (the ClassData constructor).
- `maxCharacterLevel`: 40 while a held fact sets LegendaryHero (an AddMechanicsFeature), else 20.

Aspects kept on (map ticket 32):

- `keepAspectsOn`: the Build's aspects kept on, judged at the end of each level-up in the Build's order. A held toggle goes on when its group has room (`ActivatableAbility.TryStart`: the group's WeightInGroup total, this toggle's included, within `UnitPartActivatableAbility.GetGroupSize`, which is 1 plus each held IncreaseActivatableAbilityGroupSize for the group; the None group has no limit). An on toggle holds its buff (`ActivatableAbility.ReapplyBuff`, G's `ActivatableBuff`); a toggle that no longer fits is refused and its buff goes. When a toggle turns off or is gone, the buff it applied goes with what the buff gave (`ActivatableAbility.OnDidTurnOff`), and the toggle's own deactivation takes its buff away too (`ActivatableAbility.OnDeactivate`).
- `refuseAspect`: refuses an aspect once; it stays refused for the rest of the Build.

Path switches (map ticket 31):

- `pathSwitch`: a path switch, applied as the switch class's level is added. Each takes away Mythic grants and picks gained at ranks 3 and up; those still counted until now and are recorded as dropped here.
  - Gold Dragon and Swarm That Walks (`m_IsHigherMythic`): `UnitProgressionData.AddClassLevel` calls `ReplaceAllMythicLevelsWithMythicHeroLevels` when a path is held (its local IsSuitableProgression). That removes every Mythic level after rank 2 (`RemoveMythicLevel`, which drops the facts those levels gave), re-adds the Mythic Feat and Mythic Ability picks (`GetMythicSelectionsData`, where a pick that is itself a selection gives back its nested pick), and refills the ranks with Mythic Hero levels (`AddFakeLevel`).
  - Devil and Legend run through story actions (`RemoveMythicLevels`) whose counts sit in dialogue blueprints the data doesn't hold, so they follow map ticket 31's sources. Legend keeps only ranks 1–2 (GameFAQs other-mythic-paths.txt, Rust legend.ron `kept_through_rank` 2) and ends the ascensions (`map31.legendEndsAscensions`). Every switch class (Gold Dragon, Swarm That Walks, Devil, Legend) is taken at rank 8, and a later rank may hold one only right after the same switch (`map31.switchAtRank8`; the Rust planner's GLOSSARY "Path switch" and legend.ron, GameFAQs other-mythic-paths.txt "Playing a Legend" and mythic-paths.txt; the data's own branch for Legend asks for the path class at level 5, the rank-8 level-up). The data alone opens Legend from rank 3 (`PrerequisiteClassLevel` Mythic Hero 2) and Devil and Swarm at any rank after 7; the rank is the story's. The root's `m_MythicLegenFake`, the class the story's level-up takes to choose the Legend path (`LevelUpController.Commit`), is refused at any rank (`map34.legendChoiceClass`): its only gates are story state (`PrerequisiteEtude`, `MythicClassLockComponent`), and a Build takes Legend itself. Devil keeps ranks 1–7 but drops the Azata's dragon and the Aeon's gazes (Rust devil.ron `dropped_features`, `dropped_selections`; GameFAQs other-mythic-paths.txt), and is open only from the Aeon or the Azata (`map31.devilFrom`; devil.ron `from`; the data's DevilMythicClass condition requires the same in its DLC branch).
  - Gold Dragon, Swarm That Walks and Legend lose the Mythic spellbook; Devil keeps it, no longer advancing (GameFAQs mythic-paths.txt).
- `dropRanks`: `RemoveMythicLevel` down to rank 2: every Mythic class but the starting one leaves, and what ranks 3 and up gave goes, except, with `keepGeneral`, the Mythic Feat and Mythic Ability picks (`GetMythicSelectionsData`; a pick that is itself a selection gives back its nested pick instead) and what they hold. `RemoveMythicLevel` takes away what each level gave; a component's grant goes with the fact that gave it (`lose`), not with the level it came at. The starting class then stands at `startingLevel` (`AddFakeLevel`, which grants nothing).
- `recordDropped`: records the facts a switch took away, each with where it was first gained. `dropNamed`: Devil's named drops, the features and the picks in the slots of the selections (devil.ron), every rank of each and what it gave. `loseMythicSpellbooks`: the Mythic spellbook goes, a merged one's Mythic levels included (GameFAQs mythic-paths.txt).

Classes, archetypes and progressions:

- `judgeClass`: `SelectClass.Check`: the class's prerequisites, where a chosen archetype's own alignment prerequisite stands in for the class's (`BlueprintArchetype.MeetsPrerequisites`' hasPrerequisiteAlignment, which the class screen passes as ignoreAlignment).
- `addArchetypes`: `AddArchetype.Check` and `Apply`, at the class's first level (`UnitProgressionData.CanAddArchetype`). An archetype must be one of the class's `m_Archetypes` (`AddArchetype.Check`). Once added, it removes or replaces the class's spellbook (`ClassData.AddArchetype`).
- `applyClassMechanics`: `ApplyClassMechanics.Apply`: class skills (`ApplyClassSkills`: the first archetype that replaces them, else the class's), then every held progression (`ApplyProgressions`, which, like the game's ToList, walks a snapshot of the held facts).
- `updateProgression`: `LevelUpHelper.UpdateProgression`. `addFeaturesFromProgression`: `LevelUpHelper.AddFeaturesFromProgression`.

Slots and picks:

- `addSelection`: `LevelUpState.AddSelection`: the slot's index counts this selection's slots opened earlier in the level-up.
- `prohibited`: `BlueprintFeatureSelection.IsSelectionProhibited` (NoSelectionIfAlreadyHasFeature). `obligatory`: the selection's Obligatory flag, which opens its slot anyway.
- `selectionWaives`: `BlueprintFeatureSelection.IsIgnorePrerequisites`: the field, or, for the root's BasicFeatSelection, the main character's TricksterIgnoreFeatPrerequisites flag (an AddMechanicsFeature on a held fact).
- `slotWaives`: `FeatureSelectionState.IgnorePrerequisites`: this slot's or any parent slot's selection waives.
- `canSelect`: `BlueprintFeatureSelection.CanSelect` for a pick of a feature in a slot, undefined when the game allows it. A parametrized feat's slot takes its parameter (`judgePick`), and only a feature selection takes a feature. A picked selection must leave something to pick (`NestedFeatureSelectionUtils.AllNestedFeaturesUnavailable`).
- `canSelectAny`: `FeatureSelectionExtensions.CanSelectAny`, judged in the outer slot as the game does.
- `selectionPossible`: PrerequisiteSelectionPossible: an option not held whose prerequisites hold (or the selection waives them).
- `canSelectParam`: `BlueprintParametrizedFeature.CanSelect` for the parameter of a parametrized feat, undefined when the game allows it. The parameter must be one of the feat's Items. No parameter of the Disabled weapon subcategory (`WeaponCategoryExtension.HasSubCategory`). Each IParamPrerequisite (`PrerequisiteConditionForWeaponCategory.CanBeSelected`) runs its condition for its categories.
- `applyPicks`: the band's picks: each pick whose slot is open, judged in Build order; a pick refused for an unmet prerequisite is tried again after the band's other picks (guide §7 "The level-up session"). A pick of an earlier band whose slot opened only now is judged here.
- `slotBand`: `SelectFeature.CalculatePriority`: a slot's band, from its selection's `GetGroup`.
- `pausedAt`: the walk paused at an open slot (`pauseAt`).
- `tryPick`: judges a pick and, when the game allows it, applies it (`SelectFeature.Check`, then `Apply`).
- `markGearDependent` (ADR 0002): under the opt-in, a choice the game allows only because of the Gear is marked with the items it needs. It is judged again as though the worn Gear's Enhancement didn't count and the gear-dependent picks before it weren't held, with what they gave. If it fails then, its items are those its failing leaves need: for a stat leaf, the worn item whose Enhancement counts on a score the leaf reads (`scoresRead`; the largest bonus counts, as `ModifiableValue.ApplyModifiersFiltered` keeps one per descriptor); for a leaf naming a fact of a gear-dependent pick, that pick's items. Returns the items, or undefined when the choice doesn't depend on the Gear. Its `judge` has just allowed the choice, starting when `Unit.gearReads` stood at `readsBefore`; when it counted no worn Enhancement and no fact is set aside, judging again without the Gear would read the same values and allow it again, so it isn't run.
- `gearAside`: the held facts of the gear-dependent picks and what they gave (`descendants`), each with its pick's items. `countedItem`: the worn item whose Enhancement to a score counts: the largest, the first worn on a tie.
- `judgePick`: `SelectFeature.Check` for a pick, undefined when the game allows it. A parameter is only for a parametrized feat, whose Items are the only ones that carry one.
- `canSelectMythicSpellbook`: `BlueprintFeatureSelectMythicSpellbook.CanSelect`: its items are itself, with no spellbook or with one of `m_AllowedSpellbooks` that the character has (`UnitDescriptor.GetSpellbook`).
- `applyPick`: `SelectFeature.Apply`. A parametrized feat's pick fills the slot and the parameter's nested slot it opens: that nested slot takes the pick's parameter, and that pick adds the fact. A prestige class's ReplaceSpellbook pick advances that spellbook, and a Mythic path's spellbook pick merges its Mythic spells into a class spellbook (`Spellbook.AddSpecialList`), which then gains the Mythic ranks (`SelectFeature.GetReplaceSpellbook`). A progression an applied feature grants on apply runs now (`SelectFeature.ForEachAddFeatureOnApply`). Returns the fact the pick gained, if any.
- `refusePending`: a pick the level-up never applied: its slot never opened (`LevelUpState.AddSelection`), or the game refused it. Every band judges the open slots' picks from their own band on (`applyPicks`), so a refused pick has a refusal.
- `level`: the level-up under way; with `fail`, it is what the stats module reads (`StatsHost`).
- `minFeatureLevel`: `BlueprintArchetype.MinFeatureLevel`: 1 for a spellbook change, else its lowest add or remove level.

### The prerequisite judge (`check.ts`)

P says what each leaf asks; this module answers whether the character has it, porting each prerequisite component's CheckInternal and the rule that combines them (`BlueprintFeature.MeetsPrerequisites`, `BlueprintCharacterClass.MeetsPrerequisites`).

- `ALIGNMENT_BIT`: each alignment's bit (AlignmentMaskType). `MASK_NAMES`: AlignmentMaskType's named groups beside the nine alignments. `alignmentMask`: an AlignmentMaskType value as the dump writes it, raw bits or names joined by ", ". `inMask`: whether an alignment is in a mask.
- `hasAlignmentComponent`: `AlignmentExtension.HasComponent`: whether an alignment has one axis; `Neutral` is either axis neutral.
- `SlotChain`: the slot a pick is judged in (`FeatureSelectionState`), as far as prerequisites read it: the item picked in it and in the slots nested below it. `isSelectedInChildren` follows `next` (`FeatureSelectionState.IsSelectedInChildren`).
- `Context`: what a check knows besides the character: where it happens, the slot, and the blueprint whose list it is. `firstLevel` is `LevelUpState.IsFirstCharacterLevel`; `owner` is the prerequisite's OwnerBlueprint, which ClassLevelsForPrerequisites' ForSelection compares with; `selectionPossible` lets the walk pass its own CanSelect for PrerequisiteSelectionPossible, so this module doesn't judge picks itself.
- `Truth`: story state is always met, but the walk keeps it apart (`'story'`), so a condition tree can tell (map ticket 21).
- `conditionsCheck`: `ConditionsChecker.Check`, with story conditions three-valued: a tree that turns on story state alone is met, since a Refusal never cites story state (map ticket 21).
- `condition`: one condition, by type: OrAndLogic combines its checker; ContextConditionHasFact and HasFact read `factHeld`; ContextConditionCharacterClass and UnitClass read the class level against their bounds; IsUnitMythicLevel reads the Mythic rank; ContextConditionBuffRank asks for a rank above RankValue; ContextConditionIsMainCharacter holds, since a Build is always the main character (`IsMainCharacter`, map ticket 01); ContextConditionIsWeaponEquipped reads `weaponEquipped`; EtudeStatus, CompanionInParty and IsCampaign are story state. Each cites its CheckCondition.
- `suitsRange`: whether a weapon's attack type fits a range type (`WeaponRangeTypeHelper.IsSuitableAttackType`).
- `weaponEquipped`: `ContextConditionIsWeaponEquipped.CheckCondition` on the Build's Gear: the primary hand is MainHand and the secondary OffHand. The weapon test is its `CheckWeapon` (range type and category); with neither, any weapon in hand will do (`UnitEntityData.GetFirstWeapon`). Additional limbs are the natural weapons a body or a polymorph gives, which the walk doesn't hold, so a test only a limb could pass fails here. That is the data's only use (a natural weapon category, in PrerequisiteConditionForWeaponCategory), so the walk refuses those parameters, as the game does for a character without the limb. HoldInTwoHands is read as a two-handed weapon type (the grip a Build can't set).
- `conditionsHold`: a ConditionsChecker outside a prerequisite (an action's Conditional, an ActivateTrigger, a parameter check): story state counts as met there too (map ticket 21). `conditionHolds`: one condition, as a parameter check (IParamPrerequisite) runs it; story state counts as met.
- `factHeld`: whether the character holds a fact, counting daily choices: a fact a daily choice offers is held while the character holds the feature that offers it (map tickets 24 and 26). Story-granted features are never held (map ticket 25): nothing in a Build grants them.
- `lentLevels`: ClassLevelsForPrerequisites, as `PrerequisiteClassLevel.GetClassLevel` reads it: the levels other classes lend this one, for this owner.
- `prerequisiteStat`: the value a stat prerequisite reads (`PrerequisiteStatValue.GetStatValue`): a skill's base value, else the stat's permanent value, then each held ReplaceStatForPrerequisites for that stat (`ReplaceStatForPrerequisites.ResultStat`).
- `permanentValue`: ModifiableValue's PermanentValue for the stats a Build's rules read: a skill's base value (`ModifiableValueSkill.CalculateBaseValue`), an ability score's permanent value, and the class tables' attack bonus and saves (`ApplyClassMechanics.ApplyBaseStats`).
- `replaceStat`: `ReplaceStatForPrerequisites.ResultStat`, with fullValue false.
- `scoresRead`: the stats a stat prerequisite reads, down to the ability scores (for ADR 0002's marking): the stat itself, a skill's attribute (ModifiableValueSkill's BaseAttribute), and the stat a held ReplaceStatForPrerequisites swaps in.
- `LeafVerdict`: one leaf's verdict: whether it holds, and for a leaf that counts, what the character has and what it asks. `atLeast` is a counted leaf's requiring form: at least `needed`.
- `judgeLeaf`: one leaf: `Prerequisite.Check`, which takes ConsiderFulfilled as met whatever the component's flags say, and otherwise CheckInternal with its polarity applied. Each case computes the requiring form; `forbids` inverts it, as the component's own flag or type does. By leaf kind:
  - `fact`: PrerequisiteFeature and PrerequisitePlayerHasFeature; PrerequisiteNoFeature reads HasFact alone.
  - `facts`: PrerequisiteFeaturesFromList counts only on a held feature and returns at the first count that reaches Amount, so at least one is needed whatever Amount says; a deity list (P's IsDeity) needs one.
  - `classLevel`: `PrerequisiteClassLevel.GetClassLevel` (with `lentLevels`); PrerequisiteNoClassLevel.
  - `archetypeLevel`: `PrerequisiteArchetypeLevel.GetArchetypeLevel`; PrerequisiteNoArchetype.
  - `stat`: `PrerequisiteStatValue.CheckUnit`; PrerequisiteFullStatValue reads the full value, which for a Build's permanent stats is the same.
  - `characterLevel` and `mythicLevel`: UnitProgressionData's CharacterLevel and MythicLevel.
  - `alignment`: PrerequisiteAlignment. At the first level the alignment is picked in the level-up's last band, so a Build's chosen alignment stands in for it; an unchosen one leaves the Build incomplete, not impossible.
  - `proficiency`: PrerequisiteProficiency is proficient with any listed item; PrerequisiteNotProficient is not proficient with some listed item, so its requiring form is "proficient with all of them".
  - `casterType`: PrerequisiteCasterType; PrerequisiteCasterTypeSpellLevel, where no spellbook of the type (null) never reaches the level.
  - `classSpellLevel`: PrerequisiteClassSpellLevel.
  - `parametrizedFeature`: PrerequisiteParametrizedFeature (CheckInternal, CheckFeature).
  - `weaponSubCategory`: PrerequisiteParametrizedWeaponSubcategory (CheckInternal, CheckFeature): the feat held at a weapon category of the subcategory (`WeaponCategoryExtension.HasSubCategory`, `weapons.ts`).
  - `selectionPossible`: PrerequisiteSelectionPossible, false outside a slot.
  - `pet`: PrerequisitePet: a held fact's AddPet of the type.
  - `isPet`: PrerequisiteIsPet reads IsPet, which a Build never is (map ticket 01).
  - `mainCharacter`: PrerequisiteMainCharacter reads IsMainCharacter, which a Build always is (map ticket 01).
  - `story`: PrerequisiteEtude reads the story's etudes; story state is always met (map ticket 21).
  - `spellsInSpellbook`: PrerequisiteAnySpellsInSpellbook: spell-parametrized feats are out of v1 (map ticket 22), so it never holds.
  - `condition`: PrerequisiteCondition.
- `considerFulfilled`: `Prerequisite.ConsiderFulfilled`, which only PrerequisiteFeaturesFromList overrides: a deity list (P's IsDeity) at the first character level is met while the character holds no deity, since the deity is chosen in a later band.
- `Judgement`: the leaves a check judges, and those that failed.
- `meetsPrerequisites`: `BlueprintFeature.MeetsPrerequisites`: a feature at its rank cap fails unless it is a selection; then every All leaf holds and, when other leaves exist, one of them does. A progression's grant (`fromProgression`) checks only CheckInProgression leaves, and a waived slot under ExceptWhiteListed only whitelisted ones.
- `classMeetsPrerequisites`: `BlueprintCharacterClass.MeetsPrerequisites`: 20 levels at most, 10 for a prestige class, then the leaves. A passing `ForcedTrue` leaf is enough on its own, and a failing one counts for nothing: it neither refuses the class nor shows as unmet. That's the game's three-way switch, and it lets a character take a first level of Angel, Demon or Dragon Disciple, whose `ForcedTrue` leaf asks for a level already held. Features and archetypes keep the game's two-way check, where `ForcedTrue` joins `Any`.
- `archetypeMeetsPrerequisites`: `BlueprintArchetype.MeetsPrerequisites`: every All leaf, and one of the others.
- `combine`: the All/Any combination the MeetsPrerequisites methods share.
- `isSelectionType`: IFeatureSelection: the blueprint types that open a slot (`SELECTION_TYPES`).

### The character (`unit.ts`)

`Unit` is the character the walk builds: the parts of the game's UnitDescriptor that the level-up rules read and write. It keeps the game's own model (UnitProgressionData, FeatureCollection, ClassData, ProgressionData), so each rule ported onto it reads the same state its C# method reads.

- `Source`: what put a fact on the character (`Feature.SetSource`), for the record the walk keeps: `race` (`SelectRace.Apply`: the race and its features), `class` (`UnitProgressionData.AddClassLevel`: a class's progression), `progression` (`LevelUpHelper.AddFeaturesFromProgression`: a progression's level entry), `pick` (`SelectFeature.Apply`: a pick, in its slot), and `component` (a held fact's grant component, AddFacts and its kin, named by G's kind).
- `Fact`: `bp` is the blueprint itself (EntityFact's Blueprint), held so the walk needn't look it up; `rank` is Feature's Rank, raised by gaining the fact again up to the blueprint's Ranks (`Feature.AddRank`); `place` is where it was first gained; `ignorePrerequisites` is Feature's IgnorePrerequisites, set for a pick in a waived slot (`SelectFeature.Apply`).
- `ClassData`: one class's level and archetypes (the game's ClassData). Its `spellbook` is ClassData's Spellbook: the class's, unless an archetype or a ReplaceSpellbook pick changes it.
- `ProgressionData`: a held progression's level and the archetypes merged into it.
- `paramKey`: a parameter as one comparable string (FeatureParam equality); '' for none. `paramBlueprint`: FeatureParam's Blueprint, the blueprint a parameter names (a spellbook, a selection's option), if any.
- `PARAM_FIELD`: the FeatureParam field each ParameterType a Build can pick fills (`BlueprintParametrizedFeature.ExtractSelectionItems`); the spell types are out of v1 (map ticket 22). `ParamType` is a ParameterType a Build can pick a parameter for; `paramValue` reads the value a parameter sets in that field, if it sets one.
- `SKILL_ATTRIBUTE`: each skill's base attribute (the CharacterStats constructor).
- `stackingOf`: how a blueprint's fact attaches while one is held (`EntityFactsManager`'s Add, through its collection's PrepareFactForAttach). FeatureCollection: a parametrized feat holds one fact per parameter (`perParam`); any other feature is one fact whose rank gaining it again raises (`one`; `Feature.AddRank`, capped at Ranks). AbilityCollection and ActivatableAbilityCollection: each grant is a fact of its own (`separate`). BuffCollection, by the buff's Stacking: Stack is a fact of its own, Replace removes the held buff first (`EntityFactsManager.Remove`, so what it gave goes with it), and Prolong, Ignore, Poison and Summ keep the held buff (`one`: a Build's buffs are permanent, so their durations don't matter).

`Unit`'s state:

- `characterLevel` and `mythicLevel`: UnitProgressionData's CharacterLevel and MythicLevel. `classes`: ClassData, in the order each class was first taken. `spellbooks`: UnitDescriptor's Spellbooks, each spellbook's level counters (`spellbooks.ts`). `lastMythicClass`: UnitProgressionData's LastMythicClass.
- `worn`: the Gear the character wears now (UnitBody; `gear.ts`). `wornEnhancement`: the worn Gear's Enhancement bonus to each ability score, the largest per score. `countGear`: ADR 0002's opt-in, under which the worn Gear's Enhancement bonuses count in the permanent value. `gearReads`: how many times a permanent value has counted a worn item's Enhancement bonus; a judgement that counted none would judge the same without the Gear (`Walker.markGearDependent`).
- `byBlueprint` (private): FeatureCollection, each blueprint's facts; more than one only for a parametrized feat's parameters. `heldFacts`: every held fact, set aside or not (EntityFactsManager's list). `watchedByBlueprint`: `byBlueprint` for the blueprints `watched` admits only, in the same order: the facts the walk re-judges. `setAside`: facts set aside while a pick is judged without the Gear (`withoutGear`), read as not held.
- `base`: stat base values: ability scores (StatsDistribution, SpendAttributePoint) and skill ranks (SpendSkillPoint). `raceStat`: Racial modifiers that aren't a held fact's component: SelectRaceStat's +2. `classSkills`: skills made class skills by a class (`CharacterStats.AddClassSkill` from `ApplyClassMechanics.ApplyClassSkills`). `spentSkillPoints`: skill points spent so far (`LevelUpHelper.GetSpentSkillPoints`). `version` is bumped on every attach and detach, so what is read from the held facts is cached until they change.
- The constructor's `watched` says which blueprints' facts `watchedFacts` lists: the walk's conditional grants (`Walker.refresh`), so it needn't scan every held fact after every change.

`Unit`'s methods:

- `componentsOf`: every held fact's components of one type, each with its fact: the components that are on (OnTurnOn).
- `held` (private): a blueprint's held facts, without those set aside. `rank`: `FeatureCollection.GetRank`, the first fact's rank, 0 when not held. `hasFact`: the game's HasFact. `factWith`: the fact of a blueprint at a parameter (a parametrized feat's one parameter). `watchedFacts`: the held facts of the blueprints `watched` admits, in `facts` order: a snapshot. `isHeld`: whether this very fact is still held, i.e. EntityFactsManager's list holds it.
- `attach`: EntityFactsManager's Add, through its collection's PrepareFactForAttach (`stackingOf`). Returns the fact, whether it is new, and the held fact a Replace buff removed, if it left: the walk takes away what that one gave (`Walker.gain`). The rank didn't move when the fact is neither new nor raised.
- `setRank`: `Feature.SetRank`: the rank, clamped to 1 and the blueprint's Ranks (the fact reapplies, as on any rank change); says whether it moved.
- `remove`: `EntityFactsManager.Remove` of one fact, through `FeatureCollection.PrepareFactForDetach`: a fact no longer held is left alone; a rank above 1 drops by one; otherwise the fact goes. Says which happened. The walk removes through `Walker.lose`, which also takes away what the fact's components gave.
- `withoutGear`: runs a judgement as though the Gear didn't count and the set-aside facts weren't held: how ADR 0002's marking asks whether a choice needs the Gear (`Walker.markGearDependent`). Nothing changes for good.
- `weaponIn`: the weapon type of the weapon in a hand (UnitBody's HandSlot), or undefined for an empty hand or a shield.
- Classes: `classData`; `classLevel` (`UnitProgressionData.GetClassLevel`); `progressionData` (`UnitProgressionData.SureProgressionData`).
- Stats: `permanent` is `ModifiableValueAttributeStat.CalculatePermanentValue`: the base score plus the permanent modifiers, which are the racial ones and a worn item's Enhancement (a permanent modifier, `IsPermanent`), the latter only under the Gear opt-in (ADR 0002). The racial modifiers are the held facts' Racial AddStatBonus (Value scaled by the fact's rank, its OnTurnOn), AddStatBonusIfHasFact (`ShouldApplyBonus`, `Update`) and AddContextStatBonus (Value × Multiplier, at least Minimal when HasMinimal, its OnTurnOn), and SelectRaceStat's.
- `contextValue` (private): `ContextValue.Calculate` for the two kinds a kept Racial bonus uses: a constant (Simple), or the rank of the fact's ContextRankConfig of the same AbilityRankType (Rank, through MechanicsContext's indexer).
- `rankOf`: `ContextRankConfig.GetValue` for the two kept base types: ClassLevel, the listed classes' levels (or every other class's, with `m_ExceptClasses`), and FeatureList, one for each listed feature the character holds (`GetBaseValue`); then `ApplyProgression` and `ApplyMinMax`.
- `modifier`: ModifiableValueAttributeStat's Bonus on the permanent value: value / 2 − 5.
- `skillBase`: `ModifiableValueSkill.CalculateBaseValue`: the ranks, plus 3 for a class skill with a rank, plus the base attribute's bonus.
- `skillAttribute`: ModifiableValueSkill's BaseAttribute: the skill's own attribute, or the one a held ReplaceStatBaseAttribute names (`ReplaceBaseAttribute`, `CheckAttribute`), only when its bonus is higher under ReplaceIfHigher.
- `isClassSkill`: ModifiableValueSkill's ClassSkill: a class made it one (`ApplyClassMechanics.ApplyClassSkills`), or a held fact's AddClassSkill (`ClassSkillRetain`) or AddBackgroundClassSkill (`AddBackgroundSkillSource`) does.
- `weaponProficiencies`: UnitProficiency: the weapon categories a character starts proficient with (its `m_WeaponProficiencies` initializer), plus each held fact's AddProficiencies (for its race only, when restricted), AddBackgroundWeaponProficiency and KensaiChosenWeapon (its OnActivate: the chosen weapon's own category). `armorProficiencies`: UnitProficiency: `None` to start with (`m_ArmorProficiencies`), plus AddProficiencies and AddBackgroundArmorProficiency. `raceAllows` (private): AddProficiencies' OnTurnOn applies with no restriction, or for the character's race.
- `tableStat`: `ApplyClassMechanics.ApplyBaseStats`: each class adds its table's value at its level, from the first archetype that replaces the table, else the class's own (ClassData's BaseAttackBonus and the saves).
- `progression`: a held progression blueprint, when the GUID names one.
- `applyProgression`: `ContextRankConfig.ApplyProgression` (C# integer division truncates, hence `Math.trunc`).

### Stats (`stats.ts`)

Ability scores and skill ranks (guide §7 "Ability scores", "Skill ranks"): the point buy, the race's chosen +2, the ability increases and the skill points, each ported from its level-up action.

- `StatsHost`: what the stats rules read and write of the walk.
- `STAT_VALUE_COSTS`: StatsDistribution's `s_StatValueCosts`, a score's cost from 0 to 24.
- `POINT_BUY`: the point buy's budget: the LevelUpState constructor starts StatsDistribution at 25 for the main character.
- `attributeBonus`: an ability score's bonus, `ModifiableValueAttributeStat.Bonus`: half the score rounded down, minus 5. The design system's StatBlock shows it.
- `pointBuyCost`: StatsDistribution: the cost of a set of scores, each moved from 10 by Add and Remove.
- `scores`: `StatsDistribution.IsComplete`: every point spent, or one left that no score can take at a cost of 1. A Build may leave points unspent (it is then incomplete), but never spend more than the budget or leave 7–18: CanRemove stops at 7 and CanAdd at 18, each a whole point at a time.
- `raceStat`: `SelectRaceStat.Check` and `Apply`: +2 Racial to the chosen ability, for a race with SelectableRaceStat.
- `increase`: `SelectClass.Apply` grants the attribute point (every fourth character level); `SpendAttributePoint.Check` and `Apply` spend it.
- `intelligencePoints`: `LevelUpHelper.GetIntelligenceSkillPoints`: half the Intelligence modifier per level, read without enhancement, rounded up on odd levels and down on even ones (toward zero, keeping the sign).
- `skillPointsAvailable`: `ApplySkillPoints.Apply` with `LevelUpHelper.GetTotalSkillPoints`: every non-Mythic class's points per level (its archetypes' AddSkillPoints included, `ClassData.CalcSkillPoints`) and the Intelligence points of each level so far, at least one per level; plus each held AddSkillPointPerCharacterLevel's extra (its `HandleUnitCalculateSkillPointsOnLevelup` adds the next character level, one point per level so far); less what is already spent, and at least 1.
- `skills`: `SpendSkillPoint.Check` and `Apply`, one rank at a time: a skill, a point left, and ranks up to the character level.

### Spellbooks (`spellbooks.ts`)

Spellbooks as prerequisite gates (guide §7 "Spellbooks, as gates"; spells themselves are phase 2). A class's spellbook is ClassData's Spellbook; its levels come from ApplySpellbook at each class level and from AddSpellbookLevel; the caster prerequisites read its MaxSpellLevel.

- `spellbookTypes`: the component types this area reads.
- `SpellbookData`: the counters a spellbook keeps (the game's Spellbook): `base` is `m_BaseLevelInternal`, `mythic` is `m_MythicLevelInternal`, and `merged` is `m_Type` Mythic: a Mythic spell list joined it (`AddSpecialList`).
- `demand`: `UnitDescriptor.DemandSpellbook`: the spellbook's counters, made on first use.
- `classSpellbook`: the ClassData constructor and `AddArchetype`: the class's spellbook, which an archetype removes or replaces.
- `applySpellbook`: `ApplySpellbook.Apply` with `Spellbook.AddLevelFromClass`: a class level raises the class's spellbook, except at the levels its SkipLevelsForSpellProgression lists. A non-Mythic class adds a base level; a Mythic class raises a Mythic spellbook to the Mythic rank (twice it, for a standalone one).
- `count`: `BlueprintSpellsTable.GetCount`: -1 off the table; a level past its end reads the last entry.
- `maxSpellLevel`: Spellbook's MaxSpellLevel (`GetMaxSpellLevel`): the highest spell level its SpellsPerDay table gives at the caster level. CasterLevel is BaseLevel, which the table's last level caps and CasterLevelModifier shifts, plus the Mythic share, none while the rank is 3 or less and the last Mythic class has DontAddCasterLevels (`GetMythicLevelForCasterLevel`). EnableSpellLevel10 is a Mythic ability's in-play flag and no gate asks for level 10, so it is left out.
- `hasCasterType`: `PrerequisiteCasterType.CheckInternal`: some class's spellbook is arcane (or divine).
- `casterTypeSpellLevel`: `PrerequisiteCasterTypeSpellLevel.GetCasterTypeSpellLevel`: the best MaxSpellLevel among the classes' spellbooks of the caster type, leaving out the alchemist's (IsAlchemistSpellbook) and Mythic ones, spontaneous only when asked; undefined when there is none.
- `classSpellLevel`: `PrerequisiteClassSpellLevel.GetClassSpellLevel`: the class's spellbook's MaxSpellLevel; undefined without one.

### Progressions (`progression.ts`)

A progression's level and its level entries (guide §2 "Progression and level"), ported onto the walk's character.

- `calcLevel`: `BlueprintProgression.CalcLevel`. A listed class counts only when none of its archetypes is listed; then the listed archetypes count instead. `m_FeaturesRankIncrease` adds the rank of each listed feature.
- `levelEntries`: `ProgressionData.CalculateLevelEntries`: each archetype merged into the progression level by level, its removes taken out (one occurrence each) and its adds put in; an archetype's add at a level the progression lacks becomes an entry of its own.
- `canAddArchetype`: `ProgressionData.CanAddArchetype`: at most one archetype that changes the spellbook, and every remove still present in the merged entries.

### Gear (`gear.ts`)

Gear (GLOSSARY.md; map ticket 12; ADR 0002): one item in each worn place, plus a main hand and an off hand. The walk judges each item as the game does when the main character equips it (`ItemEntity.CanBeEquippedBy`, which for the player's party runs CanBeEquippedInternal: the item's EquipmentRestriction components, and proficiency for a weapon, armour or shield), and holds what a worn item gives.

- `gearTypes`: the component types the Gear area reads besides G's equipment grants.
- `WORN`: the item type each worn place takes (`EquipmentSlot.IsItemSupported`).
- `PLACE_ITEM_TYPES`: every Gear place with the item types it takes, hands included (`EquipmentSlot.IsItemSupported`, `ArmorSlot`, `HandSlot`): a weapon in either hand, a shield only in the off hand. It was the planner's `PLACE_TYPES` until it moved here with its citation; the Gear panel lists its options from it.
- `oneHanded`: `ItemEntityWeapon.CanTakeOneHand`: a melee two-hander under the FighterOnHandedGrip flag (a held AddMechanicsFeature), else neither two-handed nor double.
- `misfit`: whether an item fits its place, undefined when it fits: `EquipmentSlot.IsItemSupported`, `ArmorSlot.IsItemSupported` (body armour, whose type is armour), and `HandSlot.IsItemTypeSupported` and `IsItemSupported`: a shield only in the off hand, an off-hand weapon one-handed, and a two-handed main-hand weapon with nothing in the off hand. An item the data doesn't hold is refused as `data.unknownItem`.
- `cannotWear`: `ItemEntity.CanBeEquippedBy` for the main character, undefined when the character can wear it: `BlueprintItemEquipment.CanBeEquippedBy` (every EquipmentRestriction: EquipmentRestrictionClass, EquipmentRestrictionHasAnyClassFromList and EquipmentRestrictionAlignment, each by its CanBeEquippedBy, where an unchosen alignment leaves the Build incomplete, not impossible; EquipmentRestrictionMainPlayer always passes, since a Build is the main character, map ticket 01), then `ItemEntityWeapon`, `ItemEntityArmor` and `ItemEntityShield` CanBeEquippedInternal (the weapon's category, the armour's group unless an IgnoreArmorGroupComponent waives it, the shield's armour group).
- `wornArmorGroup`: `ItemEntityArmor.ArmorType`: the armour's group, one lighter when a mithral enchantment is on it (heavy is medium, medium is light).
- `gearEnhancement`: the worn items' Enhancement bonus to a stat, where the largest counts (`ModifiableValue.ApplyModifiersFiltered`).
- `gearOf`: the Build's Gear, place by place, in a fixed order.
- `ARMOR_GROUPS`: ArmorProficiencyGroup's members in order; ArmorProficiencyGroupFlag is 1 << index.
- `armorGroupInFlags`: `RestrictionsHelper.CheckArmor` with flags: the group's bit is in the ArmorProficiencyGroupFlag value.

### Weapons (`weapons.ts`)

The weapon categories' subcategories are a table in the game's code, not in its blueprints, so the walk carries a copy.

- `SUBCATEGORIES`: WeaponCategoryExtension's `Data` array, in its order: each category with its subcategories. `WEAPON_CATEGORIES`: every weapon category in that order.
- `hasSubCategory`: `WeaponCategoryExtension.HasSubCategory`: `None` is every category's; otherwise the table's.

### Daily choices (`daily-choices.ts`)

Daily choices (GLOSSARY.md; map tickets 24 and 26) are picks the game asks for again each day, through an ability whose AbilityApplyFact applies one of a list of facts. `UnitHelper.Replenish` refills the ability's resource on rest, and `AbilityApplyFact.Apply` replaces the ability's last pick, so none of them is part of a Build. A leaf that needs one of their options is met while the character holds the feature that offers the ability (`check.ts`'s `factHeld`).

- `DAILY_CHOICES`: the features that offer a daily choice, each with the ability it gives (its AddFacts), by asset name: Wandering Spirit (map ticket 24: ShamanWanderingSpiritFeature and UnswornShamanWanderingSpiritFeature 1 and 2), Wandering Hex (ShamanWanderingHexFeature, ShamanWanderingHexFeature2, WitchWanderingHex), Minor Spirit (UnswornShamanMinorSpiritFeature 1 to 6), Wandering Skills (PossessedShamanWanderingSkillsFeature), the Wandering Mythic Tricks (TricksterPerceptionTier3Feature: one feature offers two abilities, so its second has its own entry in `SECOND_DAILY_CHOICES`), and Mixed Bloodline (map ticket 26: MixedBloodlineFeature, MixedBloodlineFeature2).
- `SECOND_DAILY_CHOICES`: the second ability a feature in `DAILY_CHOICES` offers: TricksterPerceptionTier3Feature's second trick.
- `IN_PLAY_ABILITIES`: abilities with an AbilityApplyFact that are in-play effects, not choices (map ticket 24): Cavalier Tactician shares teamwork feats with allies, and Greater Wyrmshifter is a polymorph. Keyed by the feature that gives each.
- `offers` (private): each option a daily choice can apply, with the features that offer it. `dailyChoiceOffers`: the features whose daily choice can apply a fact.

### Failures (`failure.ts`)

What the walk records when the game refuses a choice: the rule that refused it and the values that rule compared, never wording. R (ticket 04) words each rule; the walk and its tests deal only in these.

- `Rule`: a rule that refuses a choice. A game rule is named by its method, and by the field it tests where the method tests several (`BlueprintFeatureSelection.CanSelect:Mode`); a planner rule by the decision that settled it (`adr0003…`, `map31…`, `map32…`). The rules are grouped as race, scores and skills (SelectRace, SelectRaceStat, StatsDistribution, SpendAttributePoint, SpendSkillPoint, ported in `walk.ts` and `stats.ts`); classes, ranks and archetypes; picks; Gear (`gear.ts`); and aspects kept on (map ticket 32).
- `Value`: a value a rule compared: a number, a name the game uses (a stat, an alignment, a place), or several.
- `Unmet`: a prerequisite leaf that didn't hold, with what the character has and what the leaf asks, where it counts.
- `FailedRule`: why a check refused, as data: the rule, what it found and what it needed, and the leaves that failed when it reads them. R turns it into a Refusal (GLOSSARY.md) in ticket 04.
- `Failure`: a choice of the Build that the game refuses; its `subject` is the class, archetype, race, item, aspect or picked feature.

### Areas (`areas.ts`)

The component types each area of the walk reads, besides the prerequisites (P) and the grants (G) (build ticket 02d). The allowlist's A test counts each area as a reader, so every allowlisted type is read by exactly one module.

- `selectionTypes` (area 3, selections): what closes a slot, waives a slot's prerequisites, or feeds a selection's prerequisites: NoSelectionIfAlreadyHasFeature (`Walker.prohibited`), AddMechanicsFeature (`Walker.selectionWaives`, the Trickster's TricksterIgnoreFeatPrerequisites flag), ClassLevelsForPrerequisites (`check.ts` `lentLevels`) and AddPet (`check.ts` `judgeLeaf`'s pet leaf). Their citations sit on those identifiers.
- `skillTypes`: skill ranks: class skills (`Unit.isClassSkill`) and extra skill points (`stats.ts` `skillPointsAvailable`).
- `proficiencyTypes`: proficiencies, for prerequisites and Gear (`Unit.weaponProficiencies`, `Unit.armorProficiencies`).
- `abilityScoreTypes`: ability scores: the Racial bonuses in a permanent value (`Unit.permanent`), the attribute a skill reads (`Unit.skillAttribute`) and the stat a prerequisite reads (`check.ts` `prerequisiteStat`).
- `aspectTypes`: aspects kept on (map ticket 32): the group size a toggle's group allows (`Walker.keepAspectsOn`).
- `walkAreas`: each landed area, by name, with the component types it reads.

### Coverage (`coverage.ts`)

Who reads each allowlisted component type, and who applies each grant (build ticket 02d). Two checks keep the allowlist and the modules in step, as the prerequisite check keeps the allowlist and P:

- every allowlisted component type is read by exactly one of P, G, S, a landed area, or the Gear names (`tools/importer/allowlist.test.ts`);
- every grant type G reads, and every kind of grant G reads off a blueprint's own fields, is applied by the walk (`appliedGrantTypes`, `appliedFieldGrants`) or listed in `grantsAppliedElsewhere` (`coverage.test.ts`).

This is not `tools/importer/not-needed.ts`, which lists the types the allowlist leaves out.

- `grantsAppliedElsewhere`: each grant type G reads that the walk doesn't apply, with the module that applies it and why. An entry leaves when the walk applies the type.
- `readerProblems`: what is wrong with who reads the allowlisted component types: a type no reader set holds, a type two hold, or a type a set holds that the allowlist doesn't keep. Its `readers` maps each reader (P, G, S, an area, the Gear names) to its types.
- `grantCoverageProblems`: what is wrong with who applies the grants G reads (component types and field-grant kinds alike): one the walk doesn't apply that `elsewhere` doesn't list, or a listing that G doesn't read or the walk applies.

## Design notes

- **One walk per Build.** A Build is a value nothing changes, so `walk` keeps each Build object's `Walk` (`walksByBuild`, a `WeakMap`, reused only for the same data and grant index) and nothing outside the walk changes a `Unit`. The planner's sheet, E's `allows` and the slot pickers that read the same Build share one walk: a pick in the planner walked its Build 4 times and now walks it twice (QA pass 2, measured in the browser on Jack with Gold Dragon from rank 8). Opening a pick list (about 370 ms) and a pick (about 200 ms) spend under a tenth of that walking; the rest is building and drawing the list's rows, so per-level checkpoints, which would save part of a 10 ms walk, are left out.
- **One judge.** The walk is the only place legality is decided; P and G answer their own questions and the walk combines them. A Refusal's wording is R's (ticket 04): the walk records only `FailedRule` data.
- **The game's model, unrenamed.** `Unit` keeps the game's UnitDescriptor shape, and data fields keep the dump's names (`m_AllFeatures`, `LevelEntries`), so each rule reads the state its C# method reads (ADR 0005).
- **The main character.** A Build is always the main character and never a pet or a companion (map ticket 01): IsMainCharacter checks, PrerequisiteMainCharacter and EquipmentRestrictionMainPlayer pass, PrerequisiteIsPet fails.
- **Story state** is always met but kept apart as a third truth value, so a condition tree that turns on story alone is met and a Refusal never cites story state (map ticket 21). Story-granted features are never held (map ticket 25).
- **Out of v1:** spell-parametrized feats and the spell parameter types (map ticket 22); spells themselves (phase 2).
- **Action grants** follow map ticket 23; **daily choices** map tickets 24 and 26; **path switches** map ticket 31; **aspects kept on** map ticket 32; **Gear** map ticket 12 and ADR 0002 (the opt-in and the marking); **Mythic ranks at their Rank levels** ADR 0003.
- **Re-judging conditional grants.** The game re-runs each conditional grant on the event its condition reads; the walk re-runs them all after every change that can move one, until nothing changes (at most 50 rounds).
- **Retrying picks.** Within a band, a pick refused for an unmet prerequisite is retried after the band's other picks, as the level-up session allows picks in any order (guide §7).
- **Known limits.** Natural-weapon limbs aren't held, so a weapon-equipped test only a limb could pass fails (the data's only such use is a natural weapon category, which the walk then refuses). The Sohei unlock doesn't read UnitPartWeaponTraining's other weapons, so it is stricter than the game. HoldInTwoHands is read as a two-handed weapon type.
